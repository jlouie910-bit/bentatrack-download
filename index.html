How to install

It takes about a minute. Do this on your Android phone.

  1. Tap Download for AndroidWait for the file to finish downloading.
  2. Open the fileTap the notification, or find BentaTrack_v1.1.3.apk in your Downloads or Files app.
  3. Allow the install if your phone asksAndroid may say your browser can’t install apps. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then OpenSign up with your email or phone number and set up your store.

Having trouble?

My phone says the app is blocked or unsafe

BentaTrack isn’t on the Play Store yet, so Google Play Protect may show a warning. Tap More details, then Install anyway.

The install says “App not installed”

If you have an older copy of BentaTrack, uninstall it first and install this one again. Also check that your phone has at least 300 MB of free space.

I can’t find the downloaded file

Open your phone’s Files or My Files app and look in the Downloads folder for BentaTrack_v1.1.3.apk.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. BentaTrack is for Android phones only.
